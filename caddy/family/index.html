Home media

Pick an app to open it.

  • Plex Watch films and TV shows.
  • Immich Keep and share family photos and videos.
  • Seerr Ask for a film or show to be added.

Set up the app on your phone

  1. Immich

    Install the Immich app (iPhone, Android). When it asks for the server URL, enter this address:

    https://<this-address>:2283

    Then sign in with the account the owner made for you.

  2. Plex

    Install the Plex app (iPhone, Android). Sign in with the account the owner invited. The server shows up by itself.

  3. Seerr

    There is no app. Open Seerr in your browser with the tile above. Choose to sign in with Plex.
